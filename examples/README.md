# Example prompts

Paste any of these into an agent connected to BuzzSearch.

## Pain points

- "Research what people hate about their current robot vacuum. Group the complaints and quote each one with its link."
- "Search Reddit and YouTube for why people return standing desks. Which failed solutions come up most?"

## Ad hooks in their words

- "Run a deep search on what makes dog owners switch kibble brands, then generate ten hooks for a 30 second TikTok UGC ad."
- "Use the ad-script prompt on my last search, format: Meta static headline."

## Audience research

- "Who complains about adult acne on TikTok? Tell me their age hints, what they tried before, and the words they use for the problem."
- "Read the comments on this thread and summarize the objections: https://www.reddit.com/r/SkincareAddiction/comments/..."

## Reuse past research

- "List my searches about meal kits and pull the customer_lingo quotes from the newest one, sorted by intensity."
